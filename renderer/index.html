How it works

  1. Pick the console's USB / Dante / SoundGrid interface and how many channels to listen to.
  2. Have the band play (soundcheck or line check) and press Capture — or the Space bar.
  3. Check the suggestion for each channel (click to change it) and press Edit to adjust its EQ and dynamics. Press Audition (A) to hear the presets on the desk and again to undo, then Apply — or Ctrl+Enter.

Apply sets high-pass, EQ, gate and compressor. It never changes input gain, faders or mutes.
